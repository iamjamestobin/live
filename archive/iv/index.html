Our Process

We run the whole search: pinning down the role, finding the right people, screening them and putting a shortlist in front of you.

  1. Discovery

    Uncovering purpose

    We start with one question: what do you want this hire to achieve? Your answer shapes the brief, and it's how we get to know the role well enough to find the right person.

  2. Define

    Defining scope

    We look at your goals, challenges and business, then propose a plan: which markets to approach, how long it should take and how interviews will run. You get options, and together we agree the scope, the schedule and what success looks like.

  3. Presentation

    Presenting the shortlist

    When our networking, interviewing and selection are done, you get a short list of strong candidates. Every one of them fits the brief and has a track record of adding value.

  2. Define

    Defining scope

    We form our own view of your problem and help you reach the same one. Who is involved, what methods we'll use and how long the project will take: we settle those early.
