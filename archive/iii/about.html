Overview

To understand the changes in these markets as they occur, it is imperative to have a 360 degree insight. That is why Camalyn has developed powerful networks and comprehensive relationships around the globe. If our aim is to deliver new standards of openness, flexibility and intellect then our mission is to understand completely your changing market, your immediate needs and your future ambitions

Search Methodology

It is imperative that we ensure each search undertaken has been underpinned by conducting comprehensive research. Camalyn's global networks support our applicant evaluations providing us with access to an extensive community whose personal experiences can help to provide an historical context that is otherwise lost

Systematic Consulting

Camalyn apply a systematic consulting approach to all assignments, ensuring that qualified applicants are presented to employers within agreed timeframes and that successful applicants are supported through their transition and integration into the employer's organisation. At times when we are retained by an employer we would work at a pre-arranged and a consistent rate and at all times we would guarantee the visibility of our service by agreeing terms and conditions of business prior to the commencement of the search

Applicant Support

As an applicant we will support you throughout the recruitment process and make the process surprisingly easy. We consider that excellent candidate management and communication are key to our recruitment approach. Our candidate care process - from the initial qualification of skills and experience to the final decision - is comprehensive. Our consultants continually keep you up to date Regardless of whether candidates are successful, we take an honest approach and give detailed feedback to each applicant. We communicate this information either face to face or via telephone
